Ballots cast before Election Day are published by county (by party where the state registers by party) before any vote is counted. Positive = the early electorate is more Democratic in mix than 2022's. Mix is not vote choice: registered Democrats in KY and independents everywhere cross over. The county counts also feed the vote-type model: each county's early share comes from its actual ballots, and the share of early ballots already counted is known.

Reweighted read = average of three estimates for the counties not yet counted: the race's own read so far, a county-type model and a demographic model (college %, Black %, Hispanic %, population, non-metro), both fit on all reporting counties with race fixed effects. That average backtested best across the 2020→22, 2016→20 and 2020→24 county swings. Expected headline error from the replay: about ±1.2 pts at 10% counted, ±0.6 at 25% (see BACKTEST.md). Types: Hispanic ≥30%; urban core (pop ≥750k); Black ≥35%; college suburbs (metro, ≥35% bachelor's); exurban / working metro; rural (non-metro).

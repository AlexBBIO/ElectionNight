How it works

Expected county margin = 2024 presidential county margin + the statewide swing implied by the race's final polls (or the national generic ballot, D+8.7, where no race average exists).

Vote-type adjustment: early/mail and Election Day votes lean differently and post in different orders. Each county's final margin is estimated by filling in the vote types not yet counted, using 2022 county vote-type data (or state research) and each county's actual early-ballot count.

County mix: counties not yet counted are filled with the average of three estimates (the race's read so far, a county-type model and a demographic model), the combination that backtested best on 2020→22, 2016→20 and 2020→24 swings (BACKTEST.md). Expected error ≈ ±1.8 pts at 5% counted, ±1.2 at 10%, ±0.6 at 25%.
